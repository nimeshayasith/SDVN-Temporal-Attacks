\label{app:tgn-full-sweep}

architecture sweep summarised in
\S\ref{sec:tgn-offline-validation-v2} (Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-sweep-condensed}
gives only the top validation-MCC candidates in the main text). All
configurations were trained on the same 300\,s/2361-event dataset
(Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-dataset}), 400 epochs per restart attempt, up to
5 restarts per configuration, restart target val~MCC~$\geq 0.975$.
Configurations are ranked by \textbf{validation MCC} (the pre-registered
selection criterion), with validation AUROC as the printed tie-break
column; test-set metrics are given alongside for completeness but were
not used for ranking. "Seed" is blank for runs where no seed was fixed

\paragraph{Note on duplicate-looking rows.}
The three rows for "pos\_weight=6.0 ($\gamma$/$L_{\text{link}}$ overrides
and their reference run)" report numerically identical metrics by design
— this is the finding discussed in \S\ref{sec:tgn-offline-validation-v2}
(swept-dimension finding 5): overriding $\gamma$ and $L_{\text{link}}$ at
seed=100 produced bit-identical downstream training outcomes to the
un-overridden baseline at the same seed, despite the override values
being confirmed to take effect (verified via each run's printed
$\gamma$/$L_{\text{link}}$/$W_{\max}$ header). This is retained as
evidence, not merged into one row, so the reader can see the reference
and both override outcomes side by side.

\paragraph{Reconciling the run count.}
28 total training runs were performed for this campaign: the 18
configurations in Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-sweep-full} above, plus the 10
seeds in Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-seeds-full}. Three of the 18
sweep rows (the $\gamma$ override, the $L_{\text{link}}$ override, and
their seed=100 reference) and one of the 10 seed-check rows (seed=100)
all correspond to the \emph{same} underlying $d$=128/pos\_weight=6.0
configuration at seed=100 without the \texttt{ce\_weight} override —
these are reported from independent log files
(\texttt{tgn\_log\_dim128\_pw6\_gamma250.log},
\texttt{tgn\_log\_dim128\_pw6\_llink60.log}, and
\texttt{tgn\_log\_best\_seed100.log} respectively) and are not the same
training run reused three times; their identical metrics are the finding
itself, not a labelling artefact.
