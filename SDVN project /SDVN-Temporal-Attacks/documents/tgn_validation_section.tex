\subsection{Full-Stack TGN Detector: Offline Validation Results (Full-Scale, 300\,s Dataset)}
\label{sec:tgn-offline-validation-v2}

This subsection reports the offline train/validation/test evaluation of the
Full-Stack TGN detector (\S\ref{sec:tgn-arch}, Eq.~3.25--3.27) after
regenerating the training corpus at full simulation scale
($\texttt{sim\_time}=300$\,s, $N_{\text{vehicles}}=200$,
$N_{\text{RSUs}}=64$) and completing an extended hyperparameter and
random-seed campaign, superseding the earlier 30\,s pilot result reported in
\S\ref{sec:tgn-offline-validation}.

\paragraph{Compute environment.}
Training was performed with PyTorch 2.12.1 (CUDA 13.0 build) on an NVIDIA
GeForce RTX~5090 (32~GB). A total of 28 training runs were performed:
18 distinct hyperparameter/architecture configurations (the sweep), plus a
10-seed reproducibility check on the winning configuration identified by
that sweep (\S below). Wall-clock time per run ranged 1705--2057\,s
($\approx$28.4--34.3\,min), averaging 1808.3\,s ($\approx$30.1\,min), for a
combined campaign time of $\approx$14.1 GPU-hours.

\paragraph{Model selection procedure.}
Selection was performed in two stages, \textbf{both using validation MCC
as the primary key and validation AUROC as the tie-break}, following the
same leakage-avoidance rule used in the 30\,s pilot
(\S\ref{sec:tgn-offline-validation}) — the test set was not consulted at
either selection stage:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Hyperparameter sweep.} 18 configurations were trained for
          400 epochs per restart attempt (up to 5 restarts each, restart
          target val~MCC~$\geq 0.975$), varying embedding dimension
          $d \in \{32, 64, 128\}$, \texttt{pos\_weight} override
          $\in \{4.5, 5.5, 6.0, 7.0\}$ (or left at the auto-computed
          train-ratio default), GRU layers $\in \{2, 3\}$, learning rate
          $\in \{1\times10^{-3}, 5\times10^{-4}\}$, auxiliary variant-loss
          weight \texttt{ce\_weight} $\in \{0.15, 0.3, 0.5\}$, fixed
          overrides of $\gamma$ and $L_{\text{link}}$, and a 2-way
          (TTW+BSHH vs.\ ME) variant-head ablation. Five configurations
          tied at the highest validation MCC observed (0.932); the
          AUROC tie-break favoured an unseeded run
          ($d$=32, pos\_weight=4.5, val AUROC=0.973), but since that run
          is not reproducible on demand, the tied candidate that
          \emph{is} seed-fixed — $d$=128, pos\_weight=6.0,
          \texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5, seed=100 (val AUROC=0.952, the highest
          AUROC among the seed-fixed tied candidates) — was adopted as
          the stage-1 winner, since reproducibility was a stated
          requirement for the final reported model.
    \item \emph{Reproducibility check.} A separate, earlier line of
          investigation had already produced a 10-seed sweep on
          $d$=128, pos\_weight=6.0 \emph{without} the \texttt{ce\_weight}
          override (seeds $\{1, 2, 3, 7, 13, 21, 42, 55, 77, 100\}$),
          confirming that configuration family is stable (validation MCC
          range 0.897--0.923, test MCC range 0.802--0.862 across the 10
          seeds). This is retained in
          Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-seeds} as supporting evidence for the
          stability of the $d$=128/pos\_weight=6.0 family generally, but
          the seed=100/\texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5 configuration from stage 1
          — which has a strictly higher validation MCC (0.932 vs.\ 0.923)
          — is the one reported as final.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{All results below are for $d$=128, pos\_weight=6.0,
\texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5, seed=100} (restart attempt 2/5, epoch 80),
selected by validation MCC/AUROC only. Test-set metrics are reported
purely as the resulting held-out estimate and were not used to choose
between configurations.

\paragraph{Dataset.}

SDVN simulation (\texttt{scratch/routing.cc}), with
$\texttt{sim\_time}=300\,\text{s}$, $N_{\text{vehicles}}=200$,

\paragraph{Preprocessing and features.}
Unchanged from the pilot run: the six-element feature vector
$[\tau_{\text{dev}}, c_v^{W}, \Delta s_v, \rho_v, \iota_v, \phi]$ (Eq.~3.20),
beacon interval $T_b=0.1$\,s, and the urban-derived edge-freshness decay
rate $\gamma = 310.2$ and sliding-window bound $W_{\max}=430$ (from
$L_{\text{link}}=43.0$\,s). Confirmed unchanged by the $\gamma$/$L_{\text{link}}$
override ablation (finding 5 below) — the reported model uses the
auto-derived values, not a manual override.

\paragraph{Model architecture (winning configuration).}
GRU-based per-node memory with embedding dimension $d=128$, 2 GRU layers,
2 rounds of message passing over the 3-node induced subgraph
$\{\text{reporter}, \text{link\_src}, \text{link\_dst}\}$. Per-node
truncated backpropagation-through-time window $=100$ events. Joint output
heads: binary attack-score logit and 3-way attack-family classifier
(TTW/BSHH/ME).

\paragraph{Findings by swept dimension.}
Each hyperparameter/architecture axis is discussed individually below, in
the same priority order as the original pre-300\,s calibration plan. Full
per-configuration numbers are in Appendix~\ref{app:tgn-full-sweep}.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Embedding dimension ($d \in \{32, 64, 128\}$).} No
          dimension dominates on validation MCC: five configurations tied
          at 0.932, spanning $d$=32 (two configs), $d$=64 (two configs),
          and $d$=128 (one config, the reported winner). This suggests
          $d=32$ is not, in fact, too small to separate the three attack
          families — contrary to the pre-registered hypothesis — and
          $d=128$ was retained mainly because it produced the only
          seed-fixed, reproducible member of the tied group. Training
          wall-clock time was essentially flat across dimensions
          (1705--1815\,s), so dimension is not a compute-cost trade-off
          in this pipeline.
    \item \textbf{\texttt{pos\_weight} override ($\in \{4.5, 5.5, 6.0,
          7.0\}$).} pos\_weight=6.0 and 5.5 both appear among the
          validation-MCC-tied configurations (at $d$=64 and $d$=128);
          pos\_weight=4.5 also ties at $d$=32 but underperforms at
          $d$=128 (val MCC=0.924) — indicating the optimal
          \texttt{pos\_weight} interacts with embedding dimension rather
          than being a fixed global constant. pos\_weight=7.0 was only
          tested in combination with other changes (finding 6) and
          cannot be evaluated in isolation.
    \item \textbf{ME-exclusion variant head ablation ($d$=32, TTW+BSHH
          vs.\ ME 2-way target).} This configuration ties for the
          highest validation MCC (0.932) but is unseeded. Because it
          changes the label space, its variant-metric is not directly
          comparable to the 3-way configurations (F1 computed over
          $n=334$ rows under a different target definition). It does not
          appear to offer a validation-MCC advantage over the reported
          3-way winner, so the 3-way head is retained.
    \item \textbf{Auxiliary variant-loss weight (\texttt{ce\_weight}
          $\in \{0.15, 0.3, 0.5\}$, at $d$=128, pos\_weight=6.0,
          seed=100).} This is the axis that produced the reported
          winner: raising \texttt{ce\_weight} from the 0.3 default to
          0.5 improved validation MCC from 0.923 (seed=100, default
          \texttt{ce\_weight}) to 0.932 — the single largest controlled,
          reproducible improvement found in the sweep. Lowering
          \texttt{ce\_weight} to 0.15 instead gave a lower validation MCC
          (0.906) but the best variant test-F1 macro observed in the
          entire campaign (0.673, at test MCC=0.859). This is noted as an
          alternative operating point: if variant-family identification
          is prioritised over binary attack/benign MCC in the deployed
          system, \texttt{ce\_weight}=0.15 is preferable; the reported
          model uses \texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5 because it was selected on
          validation MCC, the pre-registered primary criterion.
    \item \textbf{Fixed overrides of $\gamma$ (edge-freshness decay) and
          $L_{\text{link}}$ (sliding-window bound), at $d$=128,
          pos\_weight=6.0, seed=100.} Overriding $\gamma=250.0$ (vs.\ the
          auto-derived 310.2) and $L_{\text{link}}=60.0$\,s (vs.\ the
          auto-derived 43.0\,s, which also changes $W_{\max}$ from 430 to
          600) each produced results numerically identical to the
          un-overridden seed=100 baseline (val MCC=0.923, test MCC=0.843
          in all three cases). The override values were confirmed to
          take effect (each run's log header prints the overridden
          $\gamma$/$L_{\text{link}}$/$W_{\max}$ correctly), so this is a
          genuine finding rather than a silently-ignored flag: at this
          dataset scale, these two parameters do not measurably affect
          training outcome for the seed tested. Neither is pursued as a
          further tuning axis; the auto-derivation from $L_{\text{link}}$
          (Eq.~3.22) is retained without manual override.
    \item \textbf{Combined change: GRU layers=3, learning rate=5$\times
          10^{-4}$, pos\_weight=7.0 (all at once, $d$=128, seed=100).}
          This configuration failed outright: validation MCC collapsed
          to 0.537 (vs.\ 0.90+ for every other configuration). Each
          change was plausible in isolation, but stacking all three
          simultaneously destabilised training. Recorded as a cautionary
          finding: hyperparameter changes validated individually should
          not be combined without re-validating the combination.
    \item \textbf{Random seed.} A 10-seed check on the $d$=128,
          pos\_weight=6.0 family \emph{without} the \texttt{ce\_weight}
          override (Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-seeds}) shows validation MCC
          in the range 0.897--0.923 and test MCC in the range
          0.802--0.862 across 10 seeds — a modest spread, indicating this
          configuration family is not highly sensitive to initialisation.
          The reported winner (seed=100 with \texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5)
          sits above this entire range (val MCC=0.932), which is
          consistent with \texttt{ce\_weight}=0.5 being a genuine
          improvement over the family's default rather than a seed
          artefact, since seed=100 itself only reached val MCC=0.923
          without the \texttt{ce\_weight} change.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Note on the 0.975 target.}
Across the full campaign (18 hyperparameter/architecture configurations
$\times$ up to 5 restarts, plus the 10-seed check — 28 independent runs,
well over 100 individual restart attempts in total), the highest
validation MCC observed was 0.932, reached by five configurations
simultaneously and never exceeded. This is a substantial improvement over
the 30\,s pilot's ceiling of 0.703 (\S\ref{sec:tgn-offline-validation}),
consistent with the pre-registered prediction that increasing the event
pool would improve performance materially. However, 0.975 was not reached
by any configuration or seed. Given the breadth of this sweep — three
embedding dimensions, four \texttt{pos\_weight} values, two learning
rates, two layer depths, three loss-weighting values, and 10 random seeds
— the 0.93-ish ceiling appears to be a genuine property of the
6-dimensional feature representation and/or the 2361-event corpus, rather
than an undertuned hyperparameter. This is carried forward to
Chapter~6/limitations rather than pursued with further tuning.

\paragraph{Known limitation: ME-S1/ME-S2 still contribute no attack-labelled events at full scale.}
The 30\,s pilot's ME-S1/ME-S2 zero-attack-event issue was hypothesised to
be a dataset-scale artefact. Table~\ref{tab:tgn-val2-dataset} shows this
did \emph{not} resolve at full scale: ME-S1 and ME-S2 still contribute 0
attack-labelled events out of 2, and 0 out of 18, events respectively,
even in the 300\,s/2361-event corpus. This confirms the alternative
explanation raised at the time: these two scenarios' attacks are
intercepted by the Stage-0 crypto pre-filter (HMAC/freshness/nonce checks)
\emph{before} reaching the TGN scoring stage, and this is an architectural
property of the detection pipeline, not a symptom of insufficient
simulation length. Only controller-origin ME (ME-S3, ME-S4 — internally
fabricated, with no network-layer packet for the crypto filter to
intercept) reaches the TGN, and these two scenarios alone supply the
model's entire ME-class training signal (80 attack events each). This
continues to directly constrain the 3-way variant classifier's
performance on the ME class specifically, and is recorded as a structural
limitation of the pipeline rather than a training defect.
